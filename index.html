Interaction

Select: left-click a proposition or connection. Press Delete or Backspace to remove it.

Edit: type directly in a proposition’s text box. Right-click a proposition or connection for its edit menu. The large confidence triangle can be dragged directly on the proposition.

Connect: drag from the invisible output zone on the right edge of a proposition. Drop on another proposition or on the centerline of an existing connection.

AND junction: dropping an output onto an existing connection splits that connection and inserts a small directional AND box. Connections entering its flat left side are inputs; connections leaving its pointed right side are outputs.

OR junction: drag outward from the center dot of an existing connection and drop on a proposition or logic node. The old connection splits at that point into a small OR box with one input and multiple outputs. You can also use the proposition’s left input zone for the reverse gesture.

Meaning: ordinary connections still carry only a 0–100% confidence that their source supports their target. Logic nodes represent how multiple directed connections combine; direction is shown by which side of each directional box the wire connects to.

Move: drag a proposition from any empty area of its card outside the text box, confidence control, or connection zones.

Profiles: each profile stores independent proposition and connection confidence values.

Evidence interval: the two small markers are calculated only from incoming connected evidence. Source confidence above 50% pushes upward, below 50% pushes downward, and connection confidence controls evidence strength. The proposition’s own manual confidence does not affect the interval.
